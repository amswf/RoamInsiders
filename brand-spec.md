# Roam Insider · Brand Spec

> Updated: 2026-07-13
> Direction: independent editorial travel publication

## Identity

- Wordmark: typographic `ROAM / INSIDER`; no third-party logo is reproduced.
- Voice: concise, practical, optimistic, locally aware.
- Signature detail: a slim orange route line and numbered editorial issue marks.

## Palette

- Paper: `#F6F3EA`
- Surface: `#FFFDF8`
- Ink: `#1D2B27`
- Muted ink: `#66736E`
- Route orange: `#F45B35`
- Fresh lime: `#D7E85E`
- Sky: `#B9DDE7`
- Border: `rgba(29, 43, 39, 0.16)`

## Typography

- Display: Playfair Display with a multilingual serif fallback.
- Interface and body: Noto Sans SC with local-script fallbacks.

## Imagery

- Destination photography is used only when it identifies the place or booking context.
- Image URLs remain editable in the content back office.
- No model-authored SVG illustrations and no copied Trip.com or Traveloka interface screenshots.

## Interaction

- Content discovery is the home page's primary action.
- External booking actions stay visually distinct and open in a new tab.
- Article booking action remains fixed near the bottom edge on desktop and mobile.

## Avoid

- Generic SaaS gradients, equal-sized bento tiles, decorative statistics, fake reviews and fabricated live prices.
